Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Splotify is a local-only music player. There is no account, no server, and no cloud.

What stays on your device

  • Every audio file you import is stored only in your iPhone's local storage (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Playlists, liked songs, playback position, and settings are stored locally too.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporters.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Splotify makes zero network requests except to load its own app files the first time you open it. Your music files are never transmitted, copied, or shared.

Storage

If iOS runs critically low on space it may clear website data. Your original files in the Files app are always safe, and you can re-import them.
